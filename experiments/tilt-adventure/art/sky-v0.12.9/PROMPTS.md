# Gameplay sky clouds · v0.12.9

Built-in image generation. Transparent three-row atlas retained as cloud-atlas-master.png. The deterministic export script prepares native one-bit sprites, with distant contrast baked in, for the white gameplay sky. These replace gameplay clouds only; title and itch.io banner artwork remain separate.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: black-and-white pixel art cloud sprite atlas for a 400x240 Playdate game with floating stone roads. Create THREE distinct sophisticated volumetric cumulus cloud banks, isolated on TRANSPARENT background, stacked in THREE evenly spaced ROWS (top, middle, bottom) with generous empty space between rows. Each row contains exactly one separate wide cloud silhouette, about 3:1 width-to-height. These are distant airy clouds viewed from above at a gentle isometric angle, NOT children's doodles. Rich organic puff shapes, overlapping lobes, a few wispy trailing edges, convincing volume with white illuminated tops and restrained BLACK PIXEL DITHER shadow patterns on lower/interior lobes. Elegant compact videogame pixel clusters, careful stepped edges, only opaque pure black and pure white plus fully transparent pixels. No gray, no gradients, no antialiasing. No thick black outline surrounding the entire silhouette; interior broken contours and sparse checker dithering give detail on a WHITE gameplay background. Top row: long low windswept cloud bank. Middle: fuller clustered cumulus mound. Bottom: asymmetric wispy cloud with trailing tuft. All clouds fully visible with safe margins, no detached decorative dots outside silhouettes, no stars, no islands, no scene background, no text or labels, no panels. Art should remain readable downsampled to about 112x40 pixels per cloud, so use bold organized pixel clusters rather than noise. Output portrait or square atlas, three clearly separated horizontal rows with transparent gutters. Polished game art consistent with a charming black-and-white isometric hamster adventure.
