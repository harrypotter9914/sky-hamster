# Loading artwork

Final sources: `loading-sky-original.png`, `loading-run-original.png`. Native packing: `prepare-loading.ps1`. Runtime sequence: eight upright running poses, 12 poses/second, 30 Hz drift, cycling dots and work-based fill inside the illustrated meter. `loading-preview.gif` is exported from the native Simulator, not an illustration of an unimplemented design.

The tiled-road draft and whole-hamster rotation were rejected. A noisy plastic-ball candidate was also rejected; the final sequence uses simple glass outlines and clean solid shapes. The main title art is unchanged.

## Background prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Production bitmap background for Sky Hamster, an elegant cute Playdate 1-bit pixel art game's loading screen. Landscape 5:3 composition, ideally 1600x960, downsample-ready for 400x240. Pure black and pure white only, bold clean pixel clusters, no grey antialiasing, no tiny noisy dithering. Beautiful celestial storybook night sky, restrained small four-point stars, graceful thick-outlined curling white clouds framing the LEFT and RIGHT edges and lower corners, a beautiful crescent moon in upper right, distant tiny floating rocky islands confined to side edges. Central hero placement area x35%-65%, y15%-61% is plain solid black EMPTY space: a hamster animation will be composited there later. NO hamster or ball in this background. Below the empty hero area, y64%-77% is plain black empty space for LOADING type. A gorgeous integrated EMPTY horizontal progress-meter frame centered at x20%-80% y82%-90%, capsule silhouette, bold white double rim, dark interior, small tasteful wing/cloud ornament on either end, interior unobstructed and unfilled. Keep all pixel linework strong enough to survive at 400x240, enchanting premium indie pixel game look, careful negative space and clear hierarchy, visually crafted rather than geometric placeholders. No text, no labels, no tiles, no floor, no grid, no roadway, no checkerboard, no border around whole canvas. Create only this one usable background.

## Final animation-sheet prompt

A meticulously clean 8-frame game animation sprite sheet, 4 columns by 2 rows. Flat pixel art like a high quality Game Boy sprite. Black background. White hamster jogging inside a round glass hamster ball, facing right, upright. Eight consecutive running poses. Ball drawn ONLY using a thin clean white double circle and a single small white crescent reflection, interior black except the hamster. Ball does NOT have plastic bars, no cage, no grid. Hamster is cute round chubby white, black outlines and a simple black eye. Every ball exactly the same diameter and centered within its equal square grid cell; generous solid black empty margin all around, no touching. Smooth walk cycle, feet move, head and body mostly stay put. Absolutely pristine solid black and white shapes. No splatter, no glow, no aura, no sparkles, no dust, no noise, no textures, no grey. No text or frame labels. 2:1 canvas. Designed for crisp 96px game sprites. Finish should look like carefully hand placed pixels, elegant minimal clarity.
