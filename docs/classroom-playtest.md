# September 10: Core playtest plan

# 9 月 10 日：核心试玩计划

Assignment milestone: Test the core. Any separately issued classroom submission format still follows the instructor's requirements.

对应作业里程碑：Test the core。课堂另行发布的具体提交格式仍需按老师要求执行。

## What I want to test

## 我这次要验证什么

Is D-pad force direction, continual crank replenishment, and inertia after release understandable? Will players use better force control to avoid falls?

我想知道“方向键决定推力方向、摇柄不断补充推进力度、松手后保留惯性”是否容易理解，以及玩家是否愿意通过更好的力度控制来避免掉落。

Use Sky Hamster Core Test (0.5.5-core), starting with Course 1's short flat route. If time permits, Course 2 tests zero-force descent. Jumping and dynamic machinery are outside this round.

此次使用 Sky Hamster Core Test（0.5.5-core）。优先测试第 1 关的短平地路线；如果时间允许，再用第 2 关观察零推进下坡。跳跃和动态机关不参与本轮测试。

## Predictions before testing

## 我在测试前的预测

These are hypotheses, not obtained results.

这些是待验证的预测，不是已获得的结果。

- After a short explanation, players will understand clockwise input increases force, not directly speed.

- 简短介绍后，玩家会知道顺摇增加的是推进力度，而不是立即指定速度。

- On their first bend they may keep cranking, overshoot, then try releasing earlier.

- 第一次接近弯道时，玩家可能一直摇，发现冲过头后才尝试提前松手。

- They may expect an immediate stop on release or not understand force clearing on a high-force reversal.

- 玩家可能以为松手后会立刻停下，或者不理解高力度反向时推进条清空。

- On retry, they will reduce propulsion before corners.

- 玩家再次尝试时，会主动减少接近弯道时的推进力度。

## Playtest procedure

## 我怎样安排试玩

1. Brief goal: get the ball to the finish without falling; D-pad chooses force direction and clockwise crank adds force.

1. 简短说明目标：“把球送到终点，不要掉下去；方向键选推进方向，顺摇加力。”

2. Let players act without reminders at every bend; record hesitation, repeated failure, and changes.

2. 让玩家自行操作，不在每个转弯前提醒。记录哪里停顿、哪里反复失败、如何改变操作。

3. Allow another attempt after failure and watch for new strategies.

3. 失败后让玩家再试一次，观察是否出现新的策略。

4. Ask what the crank changes, what is unclear, and what they would do next time.

4. 结束后问：“你觉得摇柄改变了什么？哪里最难理解？你下一次会怎么操作？”

5. If time remains, use the optional slope to check that zero force does not cancel gravity.

5. 若有时间，再进入可选坡道关，看玩家是否理解零推进并不抵消重力。

Allow about 2–3 minutes initially per person as a suggested procedure, not an assignment-required duration.

建议每位玩家先给 2–3 分钟；这是本次测试安排建议，不是作业规定时长。
