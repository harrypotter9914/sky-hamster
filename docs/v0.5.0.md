# v0.5.0 — Moving machinery and combined courses

# v0.5.0 — 移动机关与综合关卡

Eight added courses bring the total to 14.

新增 8 关，当前共 14 关。

|Course<br>关卡|Content<br>内容|Main test<br>主要测试|
| --- | --- | --- |
| 07 Jump chain | Three differently sized gaps | Repeated jumps, recharging, landing speed |
| 07 Jump chain | 三段不同宽度的间隙 | 连跳、重新蓄力、落地速度 |
| 08 Timed gate | Cyclic tall barrier | Slow/wait, cross on GO |
| 08 Timed gate | 周期开关的高挡板 | 减速等待、看 GO 后通过 |
| 09 Vanishing floor | Two alternating floor panels | Disappearance, warning, jumping holes |
| 09 Vanishing floor | 两块交错开合的地板 | 路面消失、预警、跳过空洞 |
| 10 Shuttle | Ferry with docking dwell | Board, wait while coasting, disembark |
| 10 Shuttle | 带停靠时间的往返平台 | 上板、滑行等待、到站下板 |
| 11 Turn table | Half-turn long deck | Board outer end, ride, leave |
| 11 Turn table | 半圈旋转的长地板 | 外端上板、被送到另一端、离板 |
| 12 Side sweep | Lateral moving barrier | Read openings, avoid edge pushes |
| 12 Side sweep | 横向往返挡板 | 看空隙、避免被挤出边缘 |
| 13 Valley relay | Descent, barrier, gap, platform | Speed/timing over a longer route |
| 13 Valley relay | 下坡、挡板、缺口、平台 | 较长路线中的速度与时机组合 |
| 14 Sky circuit | Slopes, jumps, rotation, turns, barriers, holes | Combined flow and checkpoints |
| 14 Sky circuit | 坡道、跳跃、旋转、转弯、挡板、空洞 | 综合流程与检查点 |

The first six remain. New machinery runs automatically; zero propulsion still permits riding. Jumps inherit platform velocity. Map/selection pause machinery; retry restarts its cycle. Each type is taught only at its introduction, not repeated in combined courses.

前六关保留。新机关自动运行；停止推进仍可随移动地板运行。从移动平台跳起会继承平台当时的运动速度。地图和选关暂停机关，重试从机关周期起点重新开始。各新机关只在对应的首次介绍关显示教学，综合关不重复弹出。

UI: 14-course pagination, live mechanism positions on map, GO/WAIT barriers, and CROSS/OPENING!/HOLE floor states.

界面：14 关分页选择，地图显示机关当前位置，挡板显示 GO/WAIT，开合地板显示 CROSS/OPENING!/HOLE。

Verification: SDK build and Lua 5.4 physics/real-update regressions cover 14 courses, selection boundaries, pause, riding/leaving, inherited takeoff velocity, fast barrier contact, holes, checkpoints, and final completion. Simulator checks startup, selection page two, rotating-floor lesson/scenes. Not all courses were manually completed or hardware-profiled.

验证：SDK 编译；Lua 5.4 的物理回归和真实 update 流程检查，覆盖 14 关、选关边界、暂停、平台载球与离板、起跳继承平台运动、高速挡板碰撞、开洞掉落、检查点和最终关通关跳转。模拟器确认正常启动、第二页选关、旋转地板教学及场景显示。尚未完成所有关卡的人工通关与真机性能验证。
