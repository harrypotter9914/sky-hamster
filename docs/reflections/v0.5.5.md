# 0.5.5 | Refocus tomorrow's demonstration on the core

# 0.5.5｜我想把明天的展示重新聚焦到核心

Reviewing the assignment, I realized I had already added many courses, jumps, and mechanisms. Tomorrow required a minimum playable prototype; a polished-looking version might obscure what we were actually testing.

我回头看作业进度时，发现现在已经做了很多关卡、跳跃和机关。我开始担心：明天需要的只是一个最小可玩的 prototype，现在的版本会不会太超前、太完善，让人看不到这次到底要测什么？

I rechecked the requirements before choosing a class version. September 10 emphasized a first core test: predict player understanding, then compare observation. Complete loops, scope, and polish belonged later.

所以我想重新核对要求，再挑一个适合明天给同学试玩的版本。作业在 9 月 10 日强调第一次核心测试，先预测玩家会理解什么，再对比实际观察；完整循环、范围确定和打磨都在后面的阶段。

I narrowed the demonstration to crank propulsion and inertial control: one short flat route plus an optional slope, without jumping, moving boards, or long combinations. Performance fixes remain, and the full game stays separately preserved.

这次我把展示范围收回到摇柄推进与惯性控球。课堂版只保留一条短平地路线，另留一条坡道作为可选测试，不展示跳跃、移动板或综合长关。已有性能修复继续保留，完整版本也单独保存。

I wanted focused feedback: do people understand the force bar, expect coasting, and reduce propulsion before corners? Existing decisions explain how I arrived here; the next need was observation, not more content.

我想让明天的反馈更集中：别人是否理解推进条，是否知道松手还会滑行，会不会在弯道前主动减力。已有的思考与修改可以解释我为什么走到这里，但接下来真正需要补的是玩家观察，而不是继续添加内容。
