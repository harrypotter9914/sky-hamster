# 0.5.6 | I need to see the slowest frame

# 0.5.6：我需要看见最慢的那一帧

## 1. Short-term FPS does not establish feel

## 1. 我发现短时帧率不能代表实际手感

Hardware still occasionally paused, and initial direction input seemed unresponsive despite near-30 averages. I wanted worst-frame records and moving-camera tests.

我继续在真机玩时，还是感觉到偶尔停一下，开局的方向输入也像没有响应。前一版短时检查接近 30 FPS，不能直接说明这些问题消失。我这次希望记录最慢帧，并让相机移动起来测试。

## 2. Process only nearby content

## 2. 我提出只处理屏幕附近的内容

I proposed viewport-only rendering with margin. A comparison used 128-pixel chunks, one surrounding ring, and at most one extra preparation per frame. Chunking also stalled and missed visible cache entries in complex courses, so I did not assume it was faster.

我想到只渲染屏幕里的地图，在周围多留一点，避免处理整个地图。对照实现采用 128 像素图块，周边预取一圈，每帧最多准备一个额外图块。第一轮测试显示，分块也会卡，复杂关卡有可见缓存未命中的情况。我没有直接把“分块”当成更快的结论。

## 3. Separate lost input from zero force

## 3. 我区分输入丢失和零力度

The tutorial-closing frame discarded crank input. It now continues direction/crank processing after closing while preventing accidental A jumps. Tests cover simultaneous start and held directions without requiring another press.

检查开局流程后，我发现教学关闭的那一帧会丢弃摇柄输入。我将它改为关掉提示后继续处理这一帧的方向和摇柄，同时避免 A 误触发跳跃。回归检查覆盖同时开始和持续按住方向，不需要第二次按键。

At POWER zero, direction keys intentionally apply no propulsion. I kept that rule and added a low-force hint so players know to crank clockwise rather than suspect a broken key.

另外，POWER 为零时方向键原本就没有推进力。我保留这个设计，增加力度不足时的操作提示，让玩家知道现在需要顺摇，而不是怀疑按钮坏了。

## 4. Move first-use preparation out of gameplay

## 4. 我把首次准备工作移出游玩帧

Slope-occlusion and HUD caches now prepare on entry rather than first use. Offscreen machinery skips drawing, with margins covering rotating parts and tall barriers. Invisible machinery still moves and collides normally.

坡面遮挡缓存原来在第一次用到时生成。我将这些准备工作和 HUD 缓存提前到进入关卡时，并让屏幕外的机关跳过绘制。相机附近保留余量，旋转机关和高挡板的范围也计算在内。看不到的机关依然正常运动和碰撞，不改变玩法。

## 5. Comparison favored the whole-map cache

## 5. 我依据对照结果保留整图缓存

The first two courses' slowest updates were about 11/23 ms whole-map versus 15/26 ms chunked; complex-course averages were about 22 versus 25. I retained static whole-map caching and disabled the chunk experiment by default. Whole-map here means drawing once on entry, not redrawing the route every frame.

复测中，整图模式前两关最慢更新约 11 和 23 毫秒；分块模式约 15 和 26 毫秒。复杂关卡整图平均约 22 毫秒，分块约 25 毫秒。我保留静态整图缓存，把分块作为默认关闭的实验。这里的整图是在进入关卡时画一次，不是每帧重新绘制整条路线。

## 6. Longer testing exposed a remaining problem

## 6. 我继续延长测试，没有掩盖剩余问题

A 540-frame Course 14 test still had a roughly 313 ms spike with no candidate occluders. Occlusion alone could not explain it. Planned garbage-collection comparisons were interrupted by disconnection; unverified GC changes stayed out of the release, which was not declared stutter-free.

第 14 关延长到 540 帧后，仍出现一次约 313 毫秒的尖峰，当时球没有候选遮挡体。这说明问题还不能只归结为遮挡。我安排后续垃圾回收参数对照，但连接中断，没有得到测量结果，所以没有把未经验证的 GC 改动放进正式配置，也不把这版写成完全不卡。

## 7. Record small corrections and pending checks

## 7. 我记录小修正和待验证事项

I removed repeated empty-table allocation from no-mechanism loops. Simulator inspection found the class label crowding speed digits, so it became ROLL. Regressions and both builds completed, but sustained classroom hardware testing and installation awaited reconnection. There was insufficient evidence to change corner collision rules.

我去掉无机关循环中的重复空表分配；检查模拟器时又发现课堂标签挤到速度数字，将长标签缩为 ROLL。自动回归和两种构建检查完成，但最终课堂真机持续测试和修复版安装需要重新连接设备后继续。关于卡角，目前没有足够证据另改碰撞规则。

This remains a patch without new mechanics or courses. Numbers, sampling methods, and limits are in the [test record](../performance-v0.5.6.md).

本轮保留为小版本，不增加玩法或关卡。具体数值、采样方法和限制见 [测试记录](../performance-v0.5.6.md)。
