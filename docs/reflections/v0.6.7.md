# 0.6.7 | Keep timbre, slightly restore density

# 0.6.7：保留音色，把密度稍微调回来

I briefly considered reverting, then chose to keep the timbre and slightly increase the now-too-sparse contacts.

我一度考虑换回之前的声音，但随后决定先不换音色。当前版的接触声又太稀疏，所以我希望只提高一点密度。

Resonance and volume stay while average contact spacing changes from 135 ms to 90 ms, increasing density about half. This isolates rhythm for comparison rather than changing timbre simultaneously. Hardware listening remains decisive.

这次保留当前声音的共鸣和音量，把平均接触间隔从 135 ms 调到 90 ms，密度增加约一半。这样可以单独比较节奏变化，避免同时换音色后难以判断改动效果。最终仍以真机试听为准。

Resources and build completed, and this version installed on hardware for listening.

本次资源检查和构建完成，并已安装到真机供试听。
