# 0.6.6 | Slow rolling must not resemble electronic speech

# 0.6.6：慢速不能像密集电子语音

On hardware, the previous sound began too dense and strong, like an alien talking. Removing the recorded drop was insufficient; rhythm and resonance also changed the impression.

我在真机上听到上一版，觉得一开始就太密集，声音也太强，像外星人在说话。这个反馈说明，仅仅去掉录音中的落地声还不够，接触节奏和共鸣同样会让声音偏离预期。

I spaced contacts further apart, shortened resonance, narrowed pitch variation, and reduced volume so slow rolling began light and sparse. Parameter changes alone do not establish acceptable timbre; hardware listening is still needed.

这次把接触间隔明显拉开，缩短共鸣、缩小音高摆动并降低音量，让低速先保持轻和稀疏。我仍需要在真机上判断新的听感，不把参数调整本身当成音色已经合格。

This build installed and started on hardware for further listening.

本次构建完成并安装、启动到真机，接下来继续根据实际试听判断。
