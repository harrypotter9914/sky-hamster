# 0.6.1 | Clear labels and returns to the right place

# 0.6.1：标签应该清楚，返回应该回到来处

At checkpoints, the ball covered the purpose label as though standing on it. I wanted the label above the ball or extending over empty space with a leader back to the point.

我到达检查点时发现，小球会盖住那个显示用途的标签，看起来像踩在标签上。我希望标签能盖住球，或者伸到没有地面的方向，再指回实际点位。

Ground triggers and floating labels are separate: dots stay on the ground; text and leaders draw above the ball. Labels prefer open space off-track and choose their side during preparation to avoid sudden switches. The reviewed SAVED sign sat outside the road while the ball occupied its trigger.

我把地面触发标记和悬浮标签分开处理：地面圆点仍属于地面，悬浮文字和连接线放在球上层。标签优先伸向赛道外较空的一侧，位置方向在关卡准备时选好，避免玩的时候突然换边。实际检查点画面中，SAVED 标签位于赛道外侧，球位于地面点位上。

Selecting from the title then pressing B entered gameplay instead of returning. Return should follow origin: title to title, gameplay to the same course without losing state.

我还发现，从首页进入选关再按 B，会进入游戏，而不是回到首页。我希望返回遵循进入时的路径：从首页来就回首页，从正在玩的关卡来就继续那一关，不能回到最开始，也不能丢掉原来的状态。

Selection now records its origin. Cancel restores it without resetting position, inertia, jump height, checkpoint, propulsion, or time. Only A confirmation loads a course. Tutorial/map origins remain, and repeated opening cannot overwrite the original origin.

这次让选关记录自己的入口。取消选择只恢复原界面，不重置位置、惯性、跳跃高度、检查点、推进力度或关卡时间；只有按 A 确认关卡时才加载关卡。教学和暂停地图的入口也保留，重复打开选关不会覆盖原入口。

Checks covered both title entries, non-first-course restoration, tutorial/map return, and label depth. Existing Title/sound already served returning home, so no duplicate item was needed. This patch leaves classroom hardware on prepared 0.5.8.

我验证了主菜单的两种入口、游戏中非首关的状态恢复、教学与地图返回，并检查标签与球的绘制顺序。已有的 Title / sound 已能回到标题，不需要再增加重复菜单项。这次按小版本记录，课堂真机仍保留之前准备好的 0.5.8。

The full-game install found no Playdate COM port or drive even after retries. Build verification and deployment are separate: the package is ready; installation awaits connection.

安装这次完整版时，系统暂时无法识别 Playdate，重试后依然没有 COM 口或数据盘。我将编译验证和设备部署分开记录：包已完成，安装还需在连接恢复后继续。
