# 0.4 | Do not give players every action immediately

# 0.4｜我不想一开始就把所有操作塞给玩家

## Learning order became important

## 我开始关注学习顺序

After adding jumping, I wanted courses to teach incrementally. Earlier routes that did not need jumping should disable it and focus on propulsion, force, turning, and slopes. Introduce the ability when a gap actually requires it.

加完跳跃以后，我觉得关卡最好一关一关地教。前面那些没有涉及跳跃的路线，就先把跳跃关掉，让玩家专心熟悉推进、控制力度、拐弯和坡道。后面真正需要越过缝隙时，再告诉玩家有了新的能力。

I initially debated three early courses or more, but the principle was clear: introduce controls through course content, not display everything from the first second. This version ultimately uses four rolling-control courses before jumping in Course 5.

我讨论时还不确定前面具体应该是三关还是更多，但重点很明确：按关卡内容来引入操作，而不是从第一秒起就显示所有功能。最终这一版让前四关练习滚动控制，第五关开始进入跳跃。

## Why I changed interface wording

## 我为什么想改界面文字

CW Push and CCW Charge were not immediately clear to me. Up, Upper Right, Right, and Lower Right also left me wondering whether they named keys or on-screen ball directions.

我看到 CW Push、CCW Charge 这些字时，并不能马上理解。方向说明里的 Up、Upper Right、Right、Lower Right 也让我疑惑：它到底是在说按键，还是球在画面上的运动方向？

POWER for propulsion and JUMP for jump charge connect more directly to actions than unexplained abbreviations. The jump bar means charge, not another speed.

我希望文字直接告诉玩家要做什么。推进用 POWER，跳跃相关的条用 JUMP，比一堆需要先解释的缩写更容易对上操作。跳跃条表达的是蓄力，不是又一个速度。

Implementing a mechanic is not enough. If players must guess what each bar means, attention cannot stay on controlling the ball.

这让我意识到，机制做出来还不够。如果玩家连条表示什么都要猜，就很难把注意力放到控球上。

## Why signs needed revision

## 我为什么想改路标

Start, checkpoint, and finish text near the ground blended into routes. I wanted explicit identification rather than a few roadside words.

起点、检查点和终点的字贴在地面附近时，我觉得不太清楚，容易和路线混在一起。我想让它们更明确地表示“这里是什么点”，而不是只在路边放几个字。

Raised signs with leaders indicate their locations, with a finish flag. Players should see destinations and saved progress rather than memorize ambiguous markers.

所以这一版把文字抬成标牌，再用线指向对应位置，终点也有旗帜。我的目标是让玩家看得见要去哪里、哪里已经保存进度，而不是额外记住模糊的标记。

## Why a dedicated selection screen

## 我为什么想要独立选关界面

Selection already existed, but a click immediately switching courses felt unclear. I preferred a screen showing the available courses before choosing and confirming, making them an ordered practice sequence rather than hidden menu options.

我发现已经能选关，但觉得直接点一下就切过去不够清楚。我更希望先打开一个关卡界面，看见有哪些关，再选择并确认。这样关卡就更像一组有顺序的练习，而不是藏在菜单里的选项。

I was unsure whether a full map was necessary but kept it for route reading. Simplification alone was not a reason to remove it yet.

完整地图我一度觉得可能不一定需要，但决定先留着。它可以帮助看路线，暂时没有必要因为追求简化就删掉。

## The next issue

## 这一版之后我继续发现的问题

I wanted a new-ability notification, not repeated instruction in every jumping course. This version still repeated it later, prompting a small correction.

我想要的是“获得新技能”的提示，而不是每个跳跃关都重复同一段教学。这一版仍在后续跳跃关显示提示，和我的这个意图并不完全一致，所以接下来我又提出了一个小修正。
