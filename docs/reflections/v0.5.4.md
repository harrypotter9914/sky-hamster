# 0.5.4 | Desktop smoothness is not enough

# 0.5.4｜我不能只看电脑上流不流畅

## 1. I found hardware stutter

## 1. 我在真机上发现了卡顿

The game looked smooth on the computer but sometimes stalled on hardware. I wanted performance checked before more courses, retaining the recently corrected occlusion and collision.

电脑上的游戏看起来流畅，但真机有时候会卡。这让我觉得，接下来应该先检查整个游戏的性能，而不是继续加关卡。我想尽量优化能优化的部分，同时保留刚刚修好的遮挡和碰撞。

## 2. Work repeated every frame

## 2. 我发现有些工作每帧都在重复

Static-map occlusion bounds were recalculated every frame. Pixel alpha and visibility were read even on unobstructed flat ground.

检查后最明显的问题，是静态地图的遮挡范围每帧都在重新算。球还会被逐像素读取透明度并计算可见性，即使站在没有任何遮挡的平地上也会做这些工作。

I cached static bounds and drew the ball directly when unobstructed. The effect stayed; unnecessary work was reduced.

因此这一步的修改是缓存静态范围，并让没有遮挡的场景直接绘制球。我没有把遮挡效果删掉，而是先减少不必要的计算。

## 3. Desktop improvement was not a conclusion

## 3. 我看到电脑上的改善，但还不能据此下结论

The same desktop occlusion calculation fell from about 1.5 ms to 0.01 on an open long course and to 0.18 near a barrier. Those attractive numbers measure one desktop calculation, not a complete Playdate frame.

电脑端同一段遮挡计算，空旷长关从约 1.5 毫秒降到 0.01 毫秒，挡板附近从约 1.5 毫秒降到 0.18 毫秒。数字很好看，但它测的是电脑上的一段计算，不是 Playdate 上完整的一帧。

I continued hardware comparisons rather than letting desktop smoothness conceal device problems.

所以我继续用真机做对比，避免又用电脑的流畅掩盖硬件问题。

## 4. The first optimization was still insufficient on hardware

## 4. 我发现第一轮优化在真机上仍然不够

Short hardware calculation tests measured old flat/barrier/edge cases at about 528/599/291 ms per invocation; initial caching reduced them to about 15/60/120 ms. Pixel processing remained too heavy.

真机短时计算测试中，旧逻辑的平地、挡板和平台边缘场景约为 528、599、291 毫秒每次；第一轮缓存后约为 15、60、120 毫秒。虽然改善了，但逐像素处理仍然太重。

I replaced Lua's per-pixel rays with projected polygon masks, letting the SDK draw regions directly. Comparisons with the original visibility covered front/back overlap, slopes, low jumps, and rotating platforms.

因此下一步把 Lua 里的逐像素射线处理，换成投影后的多边形遮罩，让 SDK 直接完成区域绘制。同时对比原逻辑的可见性，检查前后遮挡、坡道、低跳和旋转平台是否仍一致。

## 5. Faster calculation does not guarantee a fast frame

## 5. 我发现计算快了，不代表整帧已经够快

The second hardware comparison fell to about 11–17 ms, but three complete-game scenes still ran only about 23–28 FPS. Occlusion was not the only cost, so one improved metric was not a stopping point.

第二轮真机计算对比降到了约 11–17 毫秒，但完整游戏的三个短时场景仍只有约 23–28 FPS。这说明性能开销不只在遮挡，不能在一个局部指标改善后就停止。

Static tracks still redrew tiles, sides, and details every frame. I cached the track on entry, moving that image during play before drawing dynamic machinery and the ball.

我继续检查静态赛道绘制，发现每帧还在重复画地砖、侧面和细节，于是把赛道改成进入关卡时生成的缓存图，游玩时只移动它，再绘制动态机关和球。

## 6. Track caching still left room for improvement

## 6. 我发现赛道缓存后还有余量要争取

The three cached scenes ran about 28.93, 27.11, and 30.08 FPS. I further reduced flat-ground occlusion filtering and cached fixed HUD text/graphics. Time and speed redraw only when their contents change.

赛道缓存后的三个场景约为 28.93、27.11、30.08 FPS，离稳定 30 FPS 已经接近，但仍有不足。我继续减少平地上的静态遮挡筛选，并缓存界面的固定文字与图形。计时和速度文字只在内容变化时重画。

I did not change movement speeds, cut courses, or lower the target frame rate to hide the issue. The goal was the same experience with less repeated work.

这个阶段没有调整移动速度或减少关卡内容，也没有通过降低目标帧率来掩盖问题。我希望保留原来的体验，减少实现中的重复劳动。

## 7. Keep each discovery, not only the successful ending

## 7. 我希望把过程中每次发现都留住

Each pass matters: what improved, what hardware exposed next, and why another approach followed. Measurement conditions and final course results are in the [performance record](../performance-v0.5.4.md).

我不只想记录“最后优化成功”，还想记录每一轮为什么继续改。第一次改善了什么、真机又暴露了什么、后来因此采用了什么办法，都值得留下。完整的测量条件与最终逐关结果见 [性能测试记录](../performance-v0.5.4.md)。

## 8. Keep remaining limits beside the improvement

## 8. 我看到最后的改善，也保留没有完全解决的情况

Short hardware checks across 14 courses averaged near 30 FPS, including barriers and rotating-platform ends. Course 13 still had one update above 33.3 ms. Short tests do not prove the game will never stutter; full play and more complex combinations still need observation.

最后对 14 关做短时真机检查，平均帧率都接近 30 FPS，挡板附近和旋转平台外端也达到了这个水平。不过第 13 关仍有一次更新超过 33.3 毫秒。我不能把短测结果写成“整个游戏永远不会卡”，后续还需要在完整游玩和更复杂的动作组合中观察。
