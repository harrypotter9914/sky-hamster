# 0.1 | First, I wanted to keep the ball from falling

# 0.1｜我想先把“控球不掉下去”做出来

## Why I wanted to make this

## 我为什么想做这个

I first thought of Hamsterball, a childhood favorite. What attracted me was not a complex background but the tension of moving along suspended routes and falling after a mistake. Ball control was the priority; ball-and-machinery puzzles also interested me, but first I needed the movement to feel tangible.

我最开始想到的是小时候很喜欢的《仓鼠球》。吸引我的不是复杂的背景，而是球沿着悬空路线移动、控制不好就会掉下去的紧张感。我想做的主要是控球不掉落，也对球和机关解谜有兴趣，但第一步还是把控球的感觉做出来。

I worried that a 3D world like the original might make a Playdate project too complex. I considered monochrome visuals, empty backgrounds, and only tracks and a ball, so I could focus on routes, inertia, and control rather than scenery.

我担心原作那样的 3D 世界放到 Playdate 上，会不会让项目太复杂。所以我考虑把它简化成黑白画面，背景留空，只保留轨道和球。这样我可以先关注路线、惯性和操作，而不是把时间都花在场景上。

## Another direction I considered

## 我考虑过的另一条路

I also considered a single-player 2D platformer: crank to climb ladders or ropes and interact with valves, keys, or doors. It offered concrete situations, but my stronger interest in Hamsterball led me to explore rolling control first.

我也想过做单人的 2D 平台跳跃：遇到梯子或绳子时摇柄控制攀爬，遇到阀门、钥匙或门时用摇柄交互。这个方向有一些具体画面，但相比之下，我对《仓鼠球》的兴趣更强，所以决定先沿着控球这个方向试。

## Finding the crank interaction

## 我在纠结什么样的摇柄操作

I did not want the crank to be optional decoration. D-pad-only movement seemed ordinary. I considered mapping crank position to direction or ball rotation, but isometric up/down/left/right need not match screen directions. If the track is diagonal too, how would players understand input?

我不希望摇柄只是一个可有可无的装饰。如果全靠方向键移动，我担心操作会太普通。我考虑过把摇柄位置映射成方向，也考虑过用它控制球的旋转，但这里有一个问题：斜视角里的上下左右，和屏幕上的上下左右不一定一致。如果轨道本身也是斜的，玩家会怎样理解输入？

I then divided the work: D-pad for direction, crank for pace, giving both hands a role. I imagined a ship or train throttle: set an amount, then accelerate or decelerate gradually, retaining coasting rather than stopping instantly.

后来我把思路转向分工：方向键负责方向，摇柄负责快慢。这样两只手都有事情做。我想过类似开船或开火车的感觉——先设定一个量，球再逐渐提速或减速；松开以后仍然能滑行，而不是立刻停住。

## Why I stopped at this version first

## 我为什么先停在这个版本

I needed something playable to judge the idea. A ball, suspended roads, fall/retry, and checkpoints let me explore direction versus speed. This was not the final solution, but a concrete prototype of the rolling-control idea.

这个阶段我需要一个能操作的东西，才能继续判断想法。先有球、悬空路线、掉落重试和检查点，就能开始感受方向和速度之间的关系。它还不是最终方案，只是把“我想做仓鼠球式控球”变成一个具体的原型。

My next question was whether the crank's speed setting would restrict the ball downhill. That became the next revision's starting point.

下一步我开始关心：如果球到了下坡，摇柄设定的速度会不会反而限制它？这成为我下一次修改的出发点。
