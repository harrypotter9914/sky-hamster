# 0.6.3 | Contact rattles, not musical tones

# 0.6.3：我要的是咯啦接触声，不是音调

The previous beeps and thuds did not fit. I clarified that a marble touching the floor should produce continuous fine rattling contacts.

我听了上一版后，觉得那个嘟嘟、咚咚的声音不符合想要的感觉。我进一步说明：玻璃珠贴着地面滚动时，我想听到的是连续、细碎的咯啦咯啦接触声。

Rather than only raise volume, I removed fixed pitch, sustained bass, and regular pulses, using irregularly spaced/weighted short contact grains with occasional close doubles. Volume behavior remains; other sounds stay unchanged.

这次不再只是提高音量，而是去掉固定音高、低沉持续音和规则脉冲，换成间隔和力度都不规则的短接触颗粒，偶尔有很近的双碰。音量曲线保留，其他声音不一起改变。

The loop grew from one to three seconds to reduce an obvious beat. Samples remain pregenerated rather than synthesized live. Peak/build checks completed; hardware listening still decides the texture.

循环从 1 秒延长为 3 秒，降低重复节拍感。采样仍提前生成，游戏中只播放，不增加实时合成。峰值与编译检查完成，最终质感继续根据真机试听判断。
