# 0.5.7 | Find pauses caused by accumulated work

# 0.5.7：我找到积累后才出现的停顿

## 1. Complex-course stutter was not an acceptable stopping point

## 1. 我没有接受“复杂关卡还会卡”作为结束

I kept investigating sudden pauses and noted that the device had remained plugged in. Windows saw it but the serial port could not open; closing the Simulator restored access. I recorded tool contention separately rather than treating it as an unplugged cable.

上一版改善了部分更新耗时，但我还是想知道为什么复杂关卡会突然停。我要求继续查原因，也重新确认真机其实一直插着。这次发现电脑可见设备，但串口不能打开；关闭模拟器后连接恢复。我把这次连接故障单独记录，避免将工具占用误认为没有插线。

## 2. Break down each frame

## 2. 我先把每帧拆开看

I timed input/physics, terrain, machinery, ball, occlusion, and UI separately. Course 14 pauses of about 312/263 ms coincided with hundreds of KB released, mostly during update. This pointed toward accumulated temporary data and collection, not blindly shrinking maps.

我将输入和物理、地形、机关、球、遮挡和界面分别计时。第 14 关的两次大停顿分别约 312 和 263 毫秒，同时内存减少几百 KB，主要时间落在更新阶段。我据此把重点转向临时数据积累后的集中回收，而不是继续盲目缩小地图。

## 3. Try idle collection and distant-occluder rejection

## 3. 我试了空闲时间回收和远处遮挡剔除

Idle-time collection and rejecting machinery that cannot be seen or occlude the ball avoid constructing unnecessary shapes. Stationary objects reuse data. Scan-test spikes disappeared, but one pass was not enough to declare completion.

我先让系统利用每帧的空闲时间回收垃圾，再将远处机关提前排除：看不到、也不可能遮住球的机关，不必先创建完整遮挡体。没有移动的机关也可以复用数据。扫图测试的几百毫秒尖峰消失了，但我没有只凭这一轮就宣布完成。

## 4. Exercise turns, jumps, and retries through real updates

## 4. 我用真实更新流程检查转向、跳跃和重试

Sustained-input tests moved the ball rather than relocating it each frame. Course 13 still paused about 323 ms, with a later 268 ms memory-release spike; barrier masking itself could cost 21–22 ms. GC settings helped but repeated temporary geometry still needed attention.

持续输入测试让球实际运动，而不是每帧搬到一个测试位置。第 13 关仍出现约 323 毫秒的停顿，后续分段测试又抓到约 268 毫秒并伴随内存释放；靠近挡板时遮罩本身也会花约 21–22 毫秒。这让我发现，GC 设置只是缓解，反复生成临时几何仍然需要处理。

Another test distinction mattered: eight crank degrees were received but produced insufficient force for immediate motion. I used 90 degrees to verify same-frame startup rather than changing intended friction and force just to pass.

测试里还有一处需要区分：8 度摇柄确实被收到，但力度太小，球没有立即移动。我将验证输入改为 90 度，确认开局同帧移动，而不是为了让测试通过去改掉原本的摩擦和力度设计。

## 5. Reuse workspaces without weakening occlusion

## 5. 我选择复用工作区，而不削弱遮挡效果

Occlusion vertices, intersections, hulls, and output now reuse buffers. Reference comparisons preserved depth, platform-edge, and jump relationships. With host GC paused, 1,000 repeated polygon calculations produced no measurable memory growth.

我把遮挡顶点、交点、凸包和输出改成重复使用的缓冲区。目标是在保持前后遮挡、平台边缘和跳跃关系的情况下减少分配。参考算法对照通过；主机暂停垃圾回收后连续计算 1000 次复用遮挡多边形，内存没有可测增长。

## 6. Record improvement with its scope

## 6. 我记录最终改善，也保留测试范围

Final hardware tests ran 450 frames each in Courses 13/14 and the short class course. Worst updates were about 32/28/14 ms, all near 30 FPS, without another hundred-millisecond spike. Same-frame classroom startup movement was confirmed.

最后一轮真机测试中，第 13、14 关和课堂短关各 450 帧，最慢更新分别约 32、28、14 毫秒，均约 30 FPS，没有再次出现几百毫秒的尖峰。课堂版开局同帧移动也得到确认。

This is patch 0.5.7: runtime fixes, no added content. Three scenes and specified actions do not guarantee every route will always be smooth. Future reported locations need reproduction. See the [performance record](../performance-v0.5.7.md).

我将这次作为 0.5.7 小版本：不增加内容，解决影响试玩的运行问题。测试覆盖的是这三个场景和指定动作，不等于所有路线永远不会卡。后续如果玩家在某个位置仍感到停顿，我会以实际复现继续检查。详细过程见 [性能记录](../performance-v0.5.7.md)。
