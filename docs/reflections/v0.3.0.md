# 0.3 | Make the crank actively matter to control

# 0.3｜我要让摇柄真正参与控球

## The issue was bypassable interaction, not insufficient speed

## 我想解决的不是速度不够，而是操作可以被绕过

If maximum propulsion plus D-pad input solved everything, the crank did not need continuing attention. I wanted a throttle to manage, not a switch set and forgotten.

上一阶段让我最在意的问题是：如果一直保持最大推进，再靠方向键处理所有情况，摇柄就失去了持续参与的必要。我希望它像需要照顾的油门，而不是设好就不管的开关。

Clockwise rotation gradually raises the bar according to accumulated rotation, not a requirement to spin particularly fast. After a short pause it retracts quickly. Players must decide when to build force and when to let it fade rather than mindlessly max it out.

我的想法是，顺着摇会把推进条一点点推高。关键是累计摇了多少，而不是必须摇得特别快。停下来之后，条经过短暂等待就快速往回缩。这样我得判断什么时候继续加力，什么时候让力度退下来，不能一直无脑摇满。

## Why I rejected direct counterclockwise braking

## 我为什么没有采用逆摇直接刹车

I considered active reverse-crank braking and accumulated reverse thrust. Both risked making danger too simple: just crank backward to slow, reducing the importance of coordinating direction and propulsion.

我考虑过让逆摇主动刹车，也考虑过累积反向推力。但我担心它们又把事情变简单：遇到危险只要反摇，就能直接减速，方向和推进之间的配合反而没有那么重要。

I wanted meaningful roles for both directions, not merely functions assigned to fill them, while preserving judgment in ball control.

我不只是想把摇柄两个方向都塞上功能。我希望两个方向各有意义，同时还能保留控球本身需要判断的部分。

## Considering track control

## 我怎样看待控制轨道这个方案

I considered stopping on a nearby button and cranking backward to rotate the track. But that could shift the core from controlling the ball to controlling the route. I preferred independently moving tracks, with players reading timing and rolling across.

我考虑过靠近某个按钮、停在上面之后，用逆摇转动轨道。但我又觉得，这会把核心从控球移到控制路线。我更喜欢轨道自己运动，玩家观察时机，然后控制球冲过去。

Track control therefore did not become this iteration's core. It clarified that I wanted attention on ball motion rather than freely rebuilding the road beneath it.

所以控制轨道没有成为这次的核心机制。它让我更明确：我要保持关注点在球的运动上，而不是让玩家随时改造脚下的路。

## Why charged jumping fit

## 我为什么接受蓄力跳跃

Counterclockwise jump charging felt appropriate, like compressing a spring. It directly supports rolling: carry speed across a gap and manage the landing.

逆摇给弹跳蓄力让我觉得合适。它有一种把弹簧往下压的感觉，而且能直接服务于控球：带着已有的速度越过缝隙，再想办法落到路上。

Jump charge and propulsion should be independent. Clockwise input only propels and does not spend jump charge. Charge can retract after input stops, but more slowly than propulsion, allowing preparation before approaching a gap.

我希望跳跃蓄力和推进力度是两件独立的事。顺摇只负责推进，不直接消耗跳跃蓄力。跳跃的条在停止蓄力后也可以回缩，但应该比推进条慢。这样我可以先准备跳跃，再安排接近缺口时的推进。

## The maximum-force reversal problem returned

## 我又遇到了“满力度反向”这个问题

Even with retraction, holding maximum and pressing reverse could immediately apply maximum opposing force as an easy brake. I wanted no extra braking bonus, but ordinary reverse force itself still required thought.

即使推进条会回缩，我仍然担心一直摇满时，按一下反方向就能立刻施加同样大的反向力，变成很方便的刹车。我不想给反方向额外刹车加成，但普通反向推力本身也需要考虑。

Waiting for the bar to reach zero before every direction change seemed rigid. I accepted a conditional alternative: a latch midway on the force bar. Above it, a large reversal clears propulsion and requires fresh cranking to rebuild force.

我想过必须等推进条完全归零才能换方向，但觉得这样可能有点怪：每一次转向都要等，会不会太僵硬？后来我接受了一个更有条件的办法：在力度条中间放一个卡扣，只有超过它的时候，大幅反向才会清空推进条，然后需要重新摇动建立力度。

I repeatedly meant force, not velocity. Fast rolling does not necessarily mean a high propulsion bar. Low-force adjustments and modest turns should not all reset it.

这里我反复强调的是“力度”，不是球的速度。球滚得快，不代表当前推进条一定很高。低力度调整和一般的小转弯不应该都被清零。

## Motion also needed to be visible

## 我也想让运动看得出来

The hamster seemed to stand still inside the ball, making rolling hard to perceive. I wanted it to rotate too so the image conveyed more than translation.

我注意到仓鼠看起来像站在球里不动，这让我难以感受到球在滚。所以我希望仓鼠也转起来，让画面能传达滚动，而不只是位置变化。

This version combines sustained propulsion, reversal latch, charged jumping, and rolling visuals. Next I needed to judge whether introducing them simultaneously left players unsure what to learn first.

这一版把持续推进、反向卡扣、蓄跳和滚动表现放到一起。我接下来需要判断的，是这些东西一开始同时出现，会不会让人不知道该先学哪一个。
