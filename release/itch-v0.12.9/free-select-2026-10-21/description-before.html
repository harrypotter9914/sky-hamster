A little hamster. A glass ball. Fifty-three roads above the clouds.

Tilt your Playdate to roll through a floating world of stone roads and curious machines. Build momentum, brake before the bend, leap across a gap, then unfold the crank to turn your next obstacle into a way forward.

For Playdate · v0.12.9 · Free, with optional donations.

Carry your momentum over the gap. Counter-tilt after landing to slow down.

Your road through the sky

Start with short balance challenges. Learn jumps, moving decks and timed hazards, then take control of rotating bridges, lifts and cannons. Wind, magnets, gates, conveyors and round spring bumpers change how you approach the road. Four long finale courses bring the mechanisms together.

  • 53 courses: learn a skill, practise it, then combine it with what came before.
  • Tilt + crank: steer the ball with the device and work machines with the handle.
  • Come back for a cleaner run: replay unlocked courses and improve your best times.
  • Help along the way: animated tutorials, checkpoints, a map and GO / WAIT signs.

Aim. Fire. Stick the landing.

Dock in a cannon, turn the crank to aim its barrel and trajectory, then press A to launch. B lets you leave without firing.

Sometimes the road turns with you

On a control deck, stop at its station and turn the crank. Watch the exit change from WAIT to GO. Press B to release, then roll onto the connected road. Automatic platforms move on their own; their timing is a different challenge.

First time playing?

  1. Select Start Game. If there is already a save, choose Continue or New Game. New Game resets campaign progress and best times.
  2. Follow the opening tutorial, choose your steering style, and hold the device still in a comfortable grip while it calibrates.
  3. Use small tilts to begin. Tilt against your movement to brake before a corner. Reach the finish to unlock the next course.

Only unlocked courses can be selected in normal play. You can return to completed courses to try for a better time.

Two ways to steer

Road is the default: right tilt moves along the road's right axis, which looks diagonal on the isometric display. Screen follows the display itself: right tilt moves toward screen-right, and up tilt moves toward screen-up.

Choose the one that feels natural. In Settings → Steering, use Left / Right to choose and A to save; B cancels. You can change it any time. Press Down during play to recenter your grip.

Install on your Playdate

  1. Click Download Now. Donations are optional; choose No thanks, just take me to the downloads to download for free.
  2. Download sky-hamster-v0.12.9.zip. It contains the playable sky-hamster-tilt.pdx game.
  3. Sign in to your Playdate account and open Sideload. Upload the ZIP directly.
  4. Connect your Playdate to Wi-Fi. Open Game Library and refresh the list (or Settings → Games → Refresh List on older system versions), then download Sky Hamster. Launch it from your game library.

Official Playdate sideloading guide. A Playdate is recommended for the motion and crank controls. To preview with the SDK Simulator, unzip the download and open sky-hamster-tilt.pdx. This is not a native Windows, macOS or Linux application.

Public beta · v0.12.9

The full 53-course campaign is included. This update adds layered, drifting pixel clouds to gameplay. Progress, completed courses, best times and settings are saved. The current checkpoint, timer and death count do not survive an app restart.

Complex courses can have frame-rate dips; the longest course took around 11 seconds to load in earlier physical-device testing. Earlier versions were tested on Playdate hardware; the latest 0.12.9 build has been checked in the Simulator and still awaits its final physical-device retest.

Found a bug or have feedback? Leave a comment with the course number, steering mode and what happened. The gameplay GIFs show real Simulator rendering with scripted inputs; they are previews, not hardware playtest footage. The cover and banner are promotional artwork.
