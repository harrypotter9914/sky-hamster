For Playdate. Download sky-hamster-v0.12.9.zip. Upload the ZIP to your Playdate account’s Sideload page, then refresh the game library on the device and install Sky Hamster. Official installation guide.

For the SDK Simulator, unzip the file and open sky-hamster-tilt.pdx. This is not a Windows/macOS/Linux executable. Motion and crank play are designed for Playdate hardware.

Keep your device still for calibration. Road steering is the new-game default; Settings → Steering lets you choose Road or Screen. Down recenters during play. New Game resets campaign progress and best times.
