Official sideloading instructions.

Getting started: choose Start Game, follow the tutorial and choose Road or Screen steering. Hold still in a comfortable grip for calibration. Tilt gently to roll; tilt against your movement to brake. Down recenters. Settings → Steering lets you change and save your preference any time.

A jumps where jumping is enabled, and fires while in a cannon. Turn the crank at a LOCKED station to work its machine; B releases. Up opens Map, Left opens Help, Right shows the Goal. New Game resets campaign progress and best times.

Simulator preview: unzip the file and open sky-hamster-tilt.pdx in the Playdate SDK Simulator. This is not a Windows/macOS/Linux executable. Motion and crank play are designed for Playdate hardware.

Public beta: complex courses may have frame-rate dips or a loading wait. This latest build still awaits its final physical-device retest. Updates retain the same game identifier; progress and settings are saved, but an app restart does not retain the current course checkpoint, timer or death count.
