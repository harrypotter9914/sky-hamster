A tilt-and-crank adventure for Playdate.

A little hamster. A glass ball. A sky full of twisting stone roads. Tilt your Playdate to roll, lean the other way to brake, leap across gaps, and use the crank to work the machines along your journey.

53 courses above the clouds

Learn one skill at a time, then combine them in longer challenges. Cross rotating bridges and moving platforms, ride lifts, aim cannons, and navigate wind, magnets, gates, conveyors and spring bumpers. Four finale courses bring the mechanisms together.

  • Two steering styles, with animated previews: Road follows the isometric road axes; Screen follows the direction you see on the display.
  • Calibrate a comfortable grip and recenter whenever you need.
  • Unlock courses as you clear them, replay completed courses and improve your best times.
  • Animated tutorials, a map, checkpoints and distinct sounds for the machines.

Controls

  • Tilt: roll; tilt against your movement to slow down.
  • Quick lift / A: jump where jumping is enabled. Lift the device quickly along the direction perpendicular to its screen; A is the button alternative.
  • Crank: operate a machine while locked to its control station, or aim a cannon.
  • A in a cannon: fire. B: release a control station or go back.
  • D-pad: Up opens Map, Left opens Help, Down recenters, Right shows the Goal.

Road steering is the default for a new game. You can switch between Road and Screen at any time in Settings → Steering. Start Game offers Continue or New Game when a journey is already saved.

Install on Playdate

Download the ZIP below, sign in to your Playdate account and upload the ZIP on the Sideload page. Refresh your Playdate game library and install Sky Hamster. Official sideloading instructions.

This is a downloadable Playdate game, not a native Windows, macOS or Linux application. A Playdate is recommended for the tilt and crank controls. The SDK Simulator can preview the unpacked .pdx.

Public beta · v0.12.9

The full current 53-course campaign is included. Free to download; donations are optional. This is an evolving public beta: complex courses can have frame-rate dips, and the longest course has taken around 11 seconds to load in earlier hardware testing. Progress, completed courses, best times and settings are saved. Mid-course checkpoints, the current timer and the current death count do not survive an app restart.
